Questions

Before you ask

How much does HubSpot actually cost in year one?

For most small teams buying Professional: the monthly subscription (priced per user — paid seats — and marketing-contact tier, billed monthly or annually), HubSpot's onboarding fee of $1,500 to $7,000 when buying direct, and any implementation cost beyond that. The assistant on this page is a free HubSpot pricing calculator for exactly this — it itemizes your year one in about two minutes, from HubSpot's published 2026 rates.

Should I buy HubSpot Starter, Professional or Enterprise?

Starter, Professional and Enterprise are HubSpot's three plans — the pricing tiers that decide most of your bill (sometimes quoted as packages with onboarding attached). Professional, for most teams this page serves — unless you can name the specific Enterprise feature you need, or Starter genuinely covers your stage (it often does for very small teams, at a fraction of the price). Overbuying the tier is the single most expensive version of overbuying HubSpot. Ask the assistant with your actual setup and it will tell you honestly which tier fits — including when the answer is the cheaper one.

Are you a HubSpot partner?

CRMdiscount isn't — the agency behind it is. This site is an independent brand run by RevOps operators whose agency is a certified HubSpot Solutions Partner. That certification is what makes the offer possible: HubSpot permits certified partners to deliver onboarding in place of its standard fee.

It also means we're not a neutral advisor, and we won't dress up as one. We benefit when you buy HubSpot and choose to work with us. That's exactly why this page is built to be verified without us: published rates, printed arithmetic, and an estimate you can take into your own rep conversation with no further contact with us.

Two ways, both in the open. If your deal qualifies and you choose them, the operators' partner agency delivers your onboarding — and any implementation work you separately ask for — as paid or partner-credited work. And deals it registers count toward its standing in HubSpot's partner program.

The chat and the estimate are free, and they don't oblige you to either. If you'd prefer to take your numbers and negotiate alone, that's a perfectly good outcome and costs you nothing.

Can you guarantee my onboarding fee will be replaced?

No, and be sceptical of anyone who does. Whether onboarding can be partner-delivered in place of HubSpot's fee depends on your tier, deal size and timing.

The difference here: the partner agency making that call is the one behind this site — so you learn where you stand in the first two minutes of a chat, not after three discovery calls.

Is this official HubSpot pricing?

No. Every figure on this page is either HubSpot's own publicly listed rate or our illustrative modelling of it. Only HubSpot can quote you. Prices, tiers and fees change — always confirm current numbers with HubSpot directly before signing anything.

Isn't the real saving just a discount on the rate?

That's the smaller half of it. In our experience the bigger loss is money spent on a plan the team never grows into — Enterprise bought for two Enterprise features, seats issued to people who only read reports, a contact tier sized to a list that's mostly dormant records.

An underused CRM costs twice: once on the invoice, and again in the adoption problem it creates. We scope for what your business needs at its current stage, so the tool gets used and the number stays defensible at renewal.
