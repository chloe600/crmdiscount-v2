Before you ask

Are you a HubSpot partner?

Yes — a certified HubSpot Solutions Partner. That's what makes the offer on this page possible: HubSpot permits certified partners to deliver onboarding themselves in place of its standard fee.

It also means we're not a neutral advisor, and we won't dress up as one. We benefit when you buy HubSpot and choose to work with us. That's exactly why this page is built to be verified without us: published rates, printed arithmetic, and an estimate you can take into your own rep conversation with no further contact with us.

We are not HubSpot, Inc. Nothing on this page is authorised, sponsored or endorsed by HubSpot, Inc.

Then how do you make money?

Two ways, both in the open. If your deal qualifies and you choose us, we deliver your onboarding — and any implementation work you separately ask for — as paid or partner-credited work for our agency. And deals we register count toward our standing in HubSpot's partner program.

The chat and the estimate are free, and they don't oblige you to either. If you'd prefer to take your numbers and negotiate alone, that's a perfectly good outcome and costs you nothing.

Can you guarantee my onboarding fee will be replaced?

No — and be sceptical of anyone who does. Whether onboarding can be partner-delivered in place of HubSpot's fee depends on your tier, deal size and timing.

The difference here: we're the partner making that call, so we can tell you where you stand in the first two minutes of a chat — not after three discovery calls.

Is this official HubSpot pricing?

No. Every figure on this page is either HubSpot's own publicly listed rate or our illustrative modelling of it. Only HubSpot can quote you. Prices, tiers and fees change — always confirm current numbers with HubSpot directly before signing anything.

Isn't the real saving just a discount on the rate?

That's the smaller half of it. In our experience the bigger loss is money spent on a plan the team never grows into — Enterprise bought for two Enterprise features, seats issued to people who only read reports, a contact tier sized to a list that's mostly dormant records.

An underused CRM costs twice: once on the invoice, and again in the adoption problem it creates. We scope for what your business needs at its current stage, so the tool gets used and the number stays defensible at renewal.

Which platforms do you actually know?

HubSpot is where most of our collective experience sits — it's the platform we're certified on and implement every week. Beyond it, our team has bought, negotiated or implemented Salesforce, Klaviyo, GoHighLevel, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, ActiveCampaign, Marketo, Braze, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Close, Attio, Keap, Freshsales and monday CRM.

We're happy to tell you when HubSpot isn't the right answer for your stage. Sometimes it isn't — and a partner telling you that is worth more than a stranger telling you that.

We already use HubSpot. Is this still useful?

Yes, but the mechanics are different — and you should know that before you start.

Onboarding is a one-time fee charged on your first purchase. At renewal there is no onboarding fee left to replace, so that lever is gone. Anyone telling an existing customer they can get it removed is selling you something that doesn't exist.

What replaces it is usually worth more anyway. Renewal is where overbuying has had a year to compound: seats issued to people who never logged in, a contact tier ratcheted up after one campaign and never brought back down, a hub bought for a project that ended in March. We run the same scoping pass against what you're actually using and show you what to cut before you re-sign.

If working with us makes sense from there, it's a different kind of engagement — helping you either genuinely use the features you're already paying for, or strip out the ones you aren't. Both directions save money.
